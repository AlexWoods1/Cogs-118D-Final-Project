Instructions

  1. Move from S to G with arrow keys or WASD. Only open corridors are legal; fog lifts as you explore.
  2. Training marks the learned route. The landmark above S maps to the first turn: teal circle = left, coral diamond = right.
  3. Later trials keep that landmark. An amber star beacon may also appear on a wall. Cues are hints only and do not open walls.
  4. Every trial is timed: make your first move before the deadline. After each goal, press Space or Next trial. Progress saves to the study database automatically; you can still download a backup log when finished.
